Dew Browser – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

Dew is a web browser for iPhone, iPad and Mac. It doesn't collect, sell or share your personal data, and it has no analytics, advertising or tracking. This page explains what stays on your device, what goes through your own iCloud account, and what is sent elsewhere only when you use a feature that needs it.

Data on your device

Your tabs, spaces, history, bookmarks, settings and website data (cookies, logins and site storage for each space) are stored on your device. Saved passwords are kept in an encrypted file whose key is in your device's Keychain. Private browsing keeps nothing after you close its tabs. You can clear history and website data at any time in Settings → Privacy.

iCloud

If you leave iCloud sync on, Dew stores your open tabs (never private ones), bookmarks and spaces in your own private iCloud database so they appear on your other devices. API keys you enter for Chat are kept in iCloud Keychain so they work on all your devices. This data is in your iCloud account, protected by Apple, and the developer of Dew cannot see it. You can turn sync off in Settings → General.

Websites you visit

Like any browser, Dew connects to the websites you open, and those websites receive what browsers normally send (such as your IP address). Their own privacy policies apply.

Features that contact other services

  • Search suggestions (optional): as you type in the address bar, what you type is sent to your chosen search engine (for example Google or DuckDuckGo) to suggest searches. You can turn this off in Settings.
  • Ad and tracker blocking: Dew periodically downloads public filter lists (EasyList and EasyPrivacy). No information about you or your browsing is sent.
  • Website icons: Dew fetches icons from the websites you visit or bookmark.
  • Chat (optional): when you ask Chat a question, your question and the text of the tabs you've shared with it (and anything you've selected on them) are sent to the AI provider you chose in Settings: Anthropic, OpenAI (ChatGPT) or another service you set up. Nothing is sent until you ask, and conversations aren't saved by Dew. The provider's own privacy policy applies to what you send it. If you choose a model running on your own device, nothing leaves the device.

Camera and microphone

Websites can ask to use your camera or microphone, for example for video calls. Dew asks you first, and only the website you allow receives access.

Children

Dew doesn't knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.
